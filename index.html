💊 Noradrenaline Calculator

Calculate infusion rate based on target dose

⚗️ Formula Noradrenaline: ml/jam = (dose × berat × 60) ÷ kepekatan
🧪 Preparation Notes: Single strength ≈4mg dalam 50ml → ±80mcg/ml. Double strength ≈ 8mg dalam 50ml → ±160mcg/ml. 4 x strength ≈16mg dalam 50ml → ±320mcg/ml.
⚠️ Important: Pastikan volume akhir 50ml untuk konsistensi kepekatan yang betul!

💊 Adrenaline Calculator

Calculate infusion rate based on target dose

⚗️ Formula Adrenaline: ml/jam = (dose × berat × 60) ÷ kepekatan
🧪 Preparation Notes (dilute in normal saline):
• Single strength: 3mg dalam 50ml → 60 mcg/ml
• Double strength: 6mg dalam 50ml → 120 mcg/ml
⚠️ Important: Pastikan volume akhir 50ml untuk konsistensi kepekatan yang betul!
💡 Dosing Range: Typical dose 0.01-0.5 mcg/kg/min
📋 Conversion: 1mg = 1000mcg • 1ml Adrenaline 1:1000 = 1mg

💊 Dobutamine Calculator

Calculate infusion rate based on target dose

⚗️ Formula Dobutamine: ml/jam = (dose × berat × 60) ÷ kepekatan
🧪 Preparation Notes:
• Single strength: 250mg dalam 50ml D5/NS → 5 mg/ml (5000 mcg/ml)
• Double strength: 500mg dalam 50ml D5/NS → 10 mg/ml (10000 mcg/ml)
• 1 ampule = 250mg/20ml
⚠️ Important: Pastikan volume akhir 50ml untuk konsistensi kepekatan yang betul!
💡 Dosing Range: Typical dose 2.5-20 mcg/kg/min
📋 Clinical Use: Inotropic support • Cardiac output augmentation
🔄 Conversion: 1 mg = 1000 mcg
